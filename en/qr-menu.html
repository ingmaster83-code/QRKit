Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I host my menu to get a link?

You can use a Google Business page, Instagram link, a page on your own website, or even a photo uploaded to Google Drive or an image host — any URL that shows your menu works.

Can I print the QR code and stick it on tables?

Yes, download it as a high-resolution PNG, print it, and place it on a table stand or sticker. Laminating it helps it last longer.

Do I need a new QR code every time the menu changes?

No, as long as the menu link (URL) itself stays the same. Just update the content at that link and the same QR code will always show the latest menu.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser and files are never sent anywhere. This keeps your personal data completely private, even in corporate security environments.

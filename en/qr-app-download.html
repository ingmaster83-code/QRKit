Frequently Asked Questions

Does one QR code work for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. The QR code points to a small redirect page that detects whether the scanning device is iOS or Android and automatically sends it to the App Store or Google Play.

Can I use only an App Store link or only a Google Play link?

Yes, entering just one link still generates a working QR code. In that case devices on the other platform will also be sent to that single link. We recommend filling in both when possible.

Will this QR code keep working long-term?

Yes, the redirect page address stays the same. You only need a new QR code if your App Store or Google Play link itself changes or is removed.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. The links you enter are embedded only in the QR code itself and are not stored on any server. The entire QR code is generated in your browser.

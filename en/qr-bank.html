Frequently Asked Questions

Does scanning this QR code send money automatically?

No. This QR code encodes your bank name, account number and holder name as plain text. Scanning it just displays that text so the payer can copy it into their banking app — there is no automatic transfer.

Can I scan it with a regular camera app?

Yes, most phone camera apps recognize QR codes automatically without any extra app.

Is my account number stored anywhere?

No. The QR code is generated entirely in your browser and nothing is sent to a server. Only share the QR image with people you trust.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser and files are never sent anywhere. This keeps your personal data completely private, even in corporate security environments.
